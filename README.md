# Awesome Etsy Tools

A curated, categorized list of reliable tools and services used by Etsy sellers.
This list focuses on official Etsy tools and well-known third-party services that integrate with Etsy.
It is intentionally curated and continuously evolving, so contributions are welcome.

## Contributing

- Add tools that are actively maintained and used by Etsy sellers.
- Prefer official websites or vendor help docs for sources.
- Keep descriptions short and factual.
- Add region limits or requirements when relevant.

## Official Etsy Tools

- [Etsy Shop Manager](https://help.etsy.com/hc/en-us/articles/360000343908-How-to-Use-Your-Dashboard-to-Manage-Your-Shop) — Official seller dashboard for listings, orders, messages, and shop stats.
- [Etsy Seller App](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/etsy-seller-manage-your-shop/id1534619962) — Official mobile app to manage orders, messages, listings, and stats.
- [Etsy Ads](https://help.etsy.com/hc/en-us/articles/360044099373-How-to-Turn-Off-Your-Etsy-Ads-Campaign) — On-platform ads for promoting listings inside Etsy.
- [Offsite Ads](https://help.etsy.com/hc/en-us/articles/360000338367-How-Etsy-s-Offsite-Ads-Work) — Etsy-run ads that promote your listings off Etsy.
- [Etsy Shipping Labels](https://help.etsy.com/hc/en-us/articles/360001967188-How-to-Purchase-Shipping-Labels-on-Etsy) — Buy and print shipping labels directly in Etsy.
- [Pattern by Etsy](https://www.etsy.com/pattern) — Standalone website builder that syncs with your Etsy listings.

## Apps and Developer Platform

- [Etsy Apps (Integrations)](https://help.etsy.com/hc/en-us/articles/360001931728-What-are-Integrations-for-Etsy-Shops) — Connect third-party apps from the Shop Manager.
- [Etsy Apps Directory](https://etsyapps.com/) — Community-run directory of Etsy apps (not affiliated with Etsy).
- [Etsy API](https://help.etsy.com/hc/en-us/articles/360025870013-What-is-Etsy-s-API) — Public API used by developers to build Etsy seller tools.

## SEO and Keyword Research

- [Marmalead](https://marmalead.com/) — Etsy-focused keyword research and listing optimization.
- [Alura](https://www.alura.io/help-center/keyword-finder) — Etsy keyword research with demand and competition data.
- [Sale Samurai](https://salesamurai.io/) — Etsy keyword research and competition analytics.
- [SpySeller](https://spyseller.com/) — Free Etsy SEO utilities like a title generator and autocomplete analyzer.

## Product Research and Market Intel

- [Alura](https://help.alura.io/en/articles/5397016-getting-started-with-keyword-research) — Research features for listings, keywords, and shops.
- [SpySeller](https://spyseller.com/) — Free Etsy research tools plus resources like a listing checklist.

- [SpySeller](https://spyseller.com/) — Fee and profitability calculators for Etsy sellers.

## Shipping Labels and Shipping Partners

- [Etsy third-party shipping partners](https://help.etsy.com/hc/en-us/articles/26654295371031-How-to-Use-a-Third-Party-Provider-to-Ship-Your-Order) — Official list of supported shipping partners by country.
- [ShipStation](https://help.shipstation.com/hc/en-us/articles/360025855432-Etsy) — Shipping workflows and label printing with Etsy order import.
- [Shippo](https://support.goshippo.com/hc/en-us/articles/4403047372699-How-to-Connect-Your-E-Commerce-Store-to-Shippo) — Shipping labels with Etsy order sync (availability depends on seller country).
- [Pirate Ship](https://www.pirateship.com/integrations/etsy) — Etsy label workflow via API; availability depends on Etsy data-sharing rules by country.
